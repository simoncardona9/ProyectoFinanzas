# Product Vision

## Problem

The current Excel workbook tracks income, expenses, taxes, invoicing, cash flow, and USD card debt. It requires manual entry and fixed formulas, is difficult to access from multiple devices, and can hide inconsistencies between cash, invoices, and obligations.

## Product goal

Create a private cloud finance application that gives the family a reliable, current view of:

- Spendable cash and projected month-end balance.
- Collected, expected, recurring, and one-off income.
- Fixed, variable, paid, pending, and deferred expenses.
- Tax and IVA money that must not be spent.
- UYU and USD debts, including exchange-rate exposure.
- Monthly history, budgets, and actionable alerts.

## Users

- Primary household administrators: enter and review finances.
- Optional household viewers: can see information without modifying it.
- Future accountant role: can review reports and export data without access to personal credentials.

## Scope boundaries

The first version is a household financial organizer, not a bank integration platform, tax-filing system, investment advisor, or automated payment service.

## Success criteria

1. A user can understand cash available, taxes reserved, and bills due in under one minute.
2. Every dashboard amount is traceable to transactions or obligations.
3. The app works securely from any authorized computer or phone.
4. New monthly data does not require changing formulas.
